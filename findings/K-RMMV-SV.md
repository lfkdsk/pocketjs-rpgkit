## Delivered importer and data contract

For an authored `System.optSideView` project, the importer now:

- validates and packages 9x6 `img/sv_actors` sheets;
- prefers `img/sv_enemies` while retaining the existing front-view fallback;
- validates and packages `Weapons1.png` through `Weapons3.png`, `States.png`
  and `Shadow2.png`;
- reports missing or malformed optional art through the existing warning and
  coverage paths instead of failing later in the cooker;
- retains authored weapon attack motion/image definitions and the system's
  magic skill-type IDs; and
- emits the additive `sideViewPresentation: "stock"` discriminator.

Implementation anchors:
`tools/rpgmaker-import/project.ts:623-710`,
`tools/rpgmaker-import/battle-data.ts:633-655`, and
`tools/rpgmaker-import/events.ts` for live Change Actor Images reporting.

The fixture project `sideview-mz` is entirely repository-authored. Its JSON,
maps, names, synthesized Ogg tones and procedural PNGs are generated by
`tests/fixtures/rpgmaker/gen-fixtures.ts`; no RTP or third-party game content is
present. Regeneration and byte comparison cover every committed fixture file.

## Delivered reducer and presentation

The reducer serializes only presentation-relevant requests and their integer
60 Hz start ticks. It classifies attacks from imported weapon motion data,
spells from authored magic skill types rather than `hitType`, items separately,
and damage versus evade reactions explicitly. Save validation accepts the
additive clocks while older saves may omit them.

The UI derives every pose from reducer-owned JSON:

- actors, their shadows, live battler sheets, weapons and state overlays paint
  at normalized stock homes on the right;
- enemies keep authored troop coordinates on the left;
- the exact 18-row motion table, stock loop pattern, 12-tick actor/weapon
  cadence, eight-tick overlay cadence, forward/return motion and entry/escape
  offsets are deterministic;
- state motion follows highest priority and database-id tie-breaking;
- weapons, actor sheets, overlays and shadows use distinct layers, while
  numeric damage/heal popups stay above their target; and
- the front-view renderer remains selected unless the additive discriminator
  is present.

Implementation anchors:
`src/rpgmaker-battle/types.ts:314-331,500-532`,
`src/rpgmaker-battle/rules.ts:2292-2313,2751-2772`,
`src/ui/rpgmaker-battle/side-view-pose.ts:9-161`, and
`src/ui/rpgmaker-battle/index.tsx:178-228,500-571`.
